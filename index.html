Who We Are

A Community Built on Blue Economy & Resilience

BlueDada Community Based Organization is a women-led, community-focused CBO rooted in Mombasa County, Kenya. We work at the intersection of sustainable livelihoods, marine conservation, and climate resilience — championing coastal communities, especially women and youth, to thrive through the blue economy.


Our name reflects our identity: rooted in the blue, driven by the Dada spirit — the sisterhood and strength of women who lead change from the shores of Kenya's coast.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To build thriving coastal communities and healthy marine ecosystems through inclusive, community-led blue economy solutions.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower coastal communities, especially women and youth, through sustainable livelihoods, marine conservation, climate resilience, local governance strengthening, advocacy, and capacity building that support healthy oceans and resilient communities.

What We Pursue

Our Core Objectives

Three pillars guide everything we do — from training sessions to the partnerships we build.

01

Inclusive Blue Economy Livelihoods

Promote sustainable and inclusive blue economy livelihoods among coastal communities, with a specific focus on women and youth empowerment.

02

Community Capacity & Conservation

Strengthen community capacity, leadership, and participation in sustainable marine resource management, environmental conservation, and climate resilience initiatives.

03

Advocacy & Knowledge Sharing

Promote community awareness, advocacy, partnerships, and knowledge sharing that support resilient coastal communities and healthy marine ecosystems.

What We Do

Our Activities

From grassroots training to international advocacy, we translate our mission into real, community-felt change.

  • 📚Community training, mentorship, and capacity-building programs for women and youth in coastal communities.
  • 💼Sustainable livelihoods, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and economic empowerment within the blue economy sector.
  • 🐠Marine conservation, environmental stewardship, and climate resilience through community participation and awareness programs.
  • 📢Advocacy, education programs, forums, workshops, and dialogues on sustainable fisheries, blue economy, and social development.
  • 🤝Partnerships with government agencies, development partners, community groups, and other stakeholders for sustainable coastal development.
  • 🌱Resource mobilization and lawful activities contributing to the achievement of the Organization's objectives.

Join Us

Membership

Open to anyone who shares our vision. We welcome individuals and community groups alike.

🙋 Who Can Join

  • Support the objectives of the Organization
  • Willing to actively participate in activities
  • Agree to abide by this Constitution
  • Aged 18 years and above

👥 Member Categories

  • Ordinary Members — individuals actively supporting our activities
  • Community Groups — women groups, youth groups, BMUs, cooperatives, and aligned CBAs

✅ Member Rights

  • Attend and vote at meetings
  • Elect and be elected to leadership
  • Access organizational information
  • Participate in all programs and activities

📌 Member Duties

  • Uphold the Constitution and objectives
  • Participate actively in meetings
  • Protect the reputation and property of the Organization
  • Promote unity, accountability, and teamwork
